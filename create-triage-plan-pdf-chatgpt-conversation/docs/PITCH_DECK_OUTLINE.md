# Eight-slide pitch outline

1. Problem and safety boundary: supports a triage nurse, never replaces one.
2. End-to-end nurse workflow and five app screens.
3. Safety gate: adult-only coverage, red flags, data quality, OOD and model-failure blocks.
4. Data/model design: intake-only features, site split, calibration, SHAP, no leakage.
5. Fast-Track policy: disabled pending zero urgent validation passes and governance approval.
6. Queue, reassessment, 3x surge, and nurse-confirmed routing.
7. Audit, privacy, local/offline posture, and future FHIR mapping.
8. Evaluation plan, known limitations, demo scenarios, and next clinical-governance steps.
